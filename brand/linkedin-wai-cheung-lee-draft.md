## About / Summary

I've always had an interest in technology — it's run underneath everything else I've done. I
trained as an Optoelectronic and Laser Engineer at Heriot-Watt University, then worked as a
Solution Architect for Hewlett-Packard and Philips (1995–2001), where I built the ECG management
system for Prince of Wales Hospital — at the time the largest deployment of its kind in the world,
serving Hong Kong's 47-hospital Hospital Authority network — and designed and implemented the
cardiology system at North District Hospital. I also worked with the territory's telecom operator
(PCCW, then Hong Kong Telecom) to connect hospitals for sharing cardiac imaging between sites,
including Grantham Hospital and Queen Mary Hospital.

Since 2010 I've founded and run the Liverpool Chinese Community Foundation — originally as a CIC,
recently converted to a CIO (charity number 1213797) — supporting early years education and the
Buddhist community in Merseyside.

Since AI agents became genuinely useful for real work (I'd point to the shift agentic tools like
Hermes triggered from around November 2025), I've been building with them properly rather than
just watching from the sidelines — using the Hermes AI digital agent to build applications and AI
bots that improve business processes, rather than just talking about the technology.

One example I can point to directly: Ruitong (ruitong.io), a tool that measures how much an LLM's
behaviour actually changes when you move it between hardware accelerators (NVIDIA, AMD, and soon
Huawei Ascend) — publishing the raw measurements, not marketing claims. That's the same standard
I apply everywhere: show the number, don't just assert it.

I'm now bringing that combination — real engineering grounding, the practical instinct you get
from running a community organisation for 15 years, and hands-on experience actually building and
shipping AI tools since late 2025 — to help small and mid-size businesses automate with AI in ways
that produce a measurable result, not a demo.
